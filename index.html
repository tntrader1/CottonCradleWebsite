Our story

A little shop with a big heart

"We wanted a place where finding something for the newest member of the family felt as special as the occasion itself."

Cotton + Cradle opened its doors in the heart of historic downtown Murfreesboro with a simple idea: thoughtfully made, heirloom-quality clothing and gifts for babies and little ones — gathered in one warm, welcoming space. From smocked daygowns to that perfect board book, every piece on our racks is chosen with care.

Behind the striped awning at 126 N. Maple, you'll find exposed brick, soft light, and shelves we've curated like we'd dress our own. Whether you're expecting, gifting, or just browsing, we'd love to welcome you in.
